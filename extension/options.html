Chrome Session Sync

Keeps the sign-ins of the sites you choose in step between your own machines. Cookies are encrypted here before they leave this browser; the hub only holds ciphertext.

1. Hub

2. Key

One random key for all your machines. It is shown once when made: save it in your password manager, then paste it on each other machine. It is kept in this browser as a non-extractable key and never in Chrome sync.

3. Sites

Sites are shared across machines through the encrypted blob; each machine still needs one click to allow them. Cookies a site meant to end when the browser closes are written with an expiry of 7 days, renewed at every sync.

    Status

    Reset replaces the blob on the hub with what this machine has. Use it after a lost key.
